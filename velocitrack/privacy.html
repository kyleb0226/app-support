Short version: VelociTrack does not collect any personal data. Everything it records stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

VelociTrack is made by Kyle Belfance, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: kyleb182610@gmail.com.

Location data

VelociTrack is a GPS speedometer and lap timer, so location is central to what it does. It asks for "While Using the App" location permission. It never asks for "Always" access.

  • What it's used for: live speed, distance, route, altitude, lap and sector timing, finish-line detection, speed runs (for example 0–60 mph) and ghost racing.
  • When it's used: while the app is open on screen, and while a recording session or speed run you started is in progress. If you lock the phone or switch apps during a recording, VelociTrack keeps recording your drive in the background. iOS shows the location indicator while it does. Recording stops when you pause or end the session. When nothing is being recorded, the app does not use location in the background.
  • Where it goes: nowhere. Location points are saved only inside the app's storage on your iPhone, as part of the sessions you choose to save. We never receive, see, sell or share your location.

What we collect

Nothing. VelociTrack has no servers and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Maps

Maps in VelociTrack are displayed with Apple's MapKit. To draw a map, your iPhone requests map imagery from Apple. This is handled by iOS under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive any of it.

Sharing and exporting

Data leaves your iPhone only when you choose to send it: for example exporting a GPX or CSV file, saving a backup to Files, sharing a ghost lap, or saving a video to Photos. What happens to it after that depends on where you send it.

Backups, retention and deletion

  • Your sessions are included in your normal iPhone and iCloud device backups, which Apple handles.
  • By default, VelociTrack also keeps rolling backup files you can see in the Files app (On My iPhone → VelociTrack). You can turn this off in Settings → Data.
  • You can delete individual sessions in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes all of its data, except backup or export files you've saved elsewhere.

Children

VelociTrack does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Driving safety

Mount your phone before you drive and don't interact with it while moving. Lap timing and speed runs are intended for closed courses. Always obey traffic laws.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If VelociTrack ever starts collecting data, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
