Privacy Policy

Support Privacy Policy All apps

Effective October 7, 2026

Short version: Ultaminder does not collect any personal data. Your reminders stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

Ultaminder is made by Kyle Belfance, an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: kyleb182610@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The reminders you create (title, notes, due date, repeat schedule and category) and your widget settings. They are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its home-screen widget so the widget can show what's coming up.

Notifications

Ultaminder asks permission to send notifications so it can remind you before things are due. These are local notifications: they are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS and are not sent through any server.

What we collect

Nothing. Ultaminder makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Backups, retention and deletion

  • Your reminders are included in your normal iPhone and iCloud device backups, which Apple handles.
  • You can delete any reminder in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Children

Ultaminder does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If Ultaminder ever starts collecting data, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
