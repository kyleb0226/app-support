Frequently asked questions

When will I be reminded?

Each reminder sends up to three notifications: 2 days before, 1 day before, and when it's due. The edit screen shows exactly which ones will be sent; any that would fall in the past are skipped. Reminders without a set time are due at 12:00 noon.

I'm not getting notifications

Open Settings → Notifications → Ultaminder and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.

How do repeating reminders work?

Turn on "Recurring reminder" and choose every N days, weeks, months or years. When you mark it done (swipe right), the next occurrence is scheduled automatically — even if you finish it early.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Ultaminder, and pick a size. In the app, the gear button lets you choose whether the widget shows your next few reminders or everything due within a number of days.

How do I edit or delete a reminder?

Tap a reminder to edit it. Swipe left on a reminder to edit or delete it.

How do I mark something done?

Swipe right on a reminder. A repeating reminder moves to its next date; a one-time reminder is removed.

Does Ultaminder collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
